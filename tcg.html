TRADING CARD GAME

VAGRIENT TCG

A VAGRIENT trading card game is in the works. It's currently in an early trial period of it's creation, however to kick things of Andrew has released an extremely limited promo holographic Sentinel card. Only 10 copies of the card will ever be in circulation, worldwide! They are the first VAGRIENT cards ever created and mark the beginning of the TCG. Continue reading to find out how you can have a chance at acquiring one.

The distribution of the 10 promo cards is being handled exclusively by - Kit Familiarr from The Card Collective AU!

The cards are being distributed at the event - SA Card Show on Sunday 18th October 2026 at the Adelaide Showgrounds, Wayville Pavilion. Kit will also be giving away other packs of cards!

Don't forget to join the friendly VAGRIENT Games Discord server and follow on socials to see what else is going on. Find links on the home page.
